It's time for your high noon duel in the small town of Crystal Point... 
but you forgot your gun! Go on the run from the cowboy as you evade wacky wild west themed dangers to make it to the stable. 
Watch out though, you'll need a saddle if you are going to skedaddle!

A first person comedy game where you avoid enemies chasing after you while trying to collect the saddle and escape the area.
